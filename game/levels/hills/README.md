# The Hills (`hills`)

A hillside suburb at golden hour. The whole level is a real sloped height field (about 22 m from Crest Drive at
the top to Valley View at the bottom, over 170 m) with the streets, cul-de-sacs, driveways, pool terrace, ditch and
park cut into it. North (-Z) is uphill; south (+Z) the hill drops away into the valley, with its town, far suburbs
and mountains.

## Layout (world metres; bounds +-110, kill_y -12)

| Area | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Crest Drive | z ~ -82, y ~ 29 | through street along the top, light traffic, houses both sides, sidewalk curbs |
| Summit Court | bulb at (8, -40) | cul-de-sac off Crest; ring curb (grind), two detached garages with driveway kickers (Driveway Launch, Colonial Kicker) |
| Pool Row | x -16..48, z -14..-1 | terrace of three back yards, a drained kidney pool in each, privacy fences between (hop them), the 5 m retaining wall behind with a ledge cap |
| The Ditch | x 60, z -70..48 | concrete channel straight down the hill: the spawn basin under the culvert, lip ledges both sides, transfers, spills into the park |
| Hillcrest Drive | west side, 210 m | the long winding bomb from Crest to Valley View (no traffic), Sunset Court off it |
| Canyon Road | east side, 169 m | the steep straight bomb (no traffic) |
| Valley Park | x -30..78, z 47..79 | skate slab: 16 m spillway quarter pipe catching the ditch, mini quarter, funbox, pyramid, two ledges, manual pad, flat rail; playground, benches, lamps |
| Secret garden | x -58..-40, z 56..71 | sunken hedged back yard with a small pool and the tape; kicker over the low hedge from the park, or the gap behind the shed |
| Valley View | z ~ 88 | through street with the school crossing (zebra, flashing beacons, crossing guard, signals that stop the cars), the overlook walk and the long guard rail (Million Dollar View) |

Spawn: in the ditch basin under the culvert, facing down the hill.

## Files

- `hills_level.gd`: `HillsLevel`: layout of streets and lots, ground shaping, roads/sidewalks/bulbs, collectibles,
  mood tuning (thinner fog, 2 shadow splits over 70 m), static mesh merging.
- `hills_terrain.gd`: `HillsTerrain`: the height field (1 m cells in the play area, coarser out to 3.4 km), shaping
  ops (corridor, flat rects/boxes/discs, holes), meshes and tiled collision.
- `hills_road.gd`: `HillsRoad`: Catmull-Rom street lines with height profiles, ribbons, sidewalks + curb grind lines.
- `hills_pool.gd`: `HillsPool`: the drained kidney pool (flow-through banks, coping rail, ramp lip ring, zones).
- `hills_channel.gd`: `HillsChannel`: the ditch.
- `hills_spots.gd`: `HillsSpots`: Pool Row, garages + kickers, Valley Park, the secret garden.
- `hills_town.gd`: `HillsTown`: overlook rail, school crossing, traffic loops, people and dogs, trees, yards
  (sprinklers, parked cars, hedges, pickets, bikes, lemonade stand), lamps, the vista (far houses, valley town and
  its lights, radio tower).
- `hills_life.gd`: `HillsLife`: sprinklers, dogs on leads, beacons, the tower blinker, car pitch on slopes, and the
  crowd's distance limits (people hidden past 50 m, shadows only within 16 m: the hillside sees a long way).
- `hills_dress.gd`: `HillsDress`: fences, pickets, retaining wall, garage body + roof, rails, signs, lamps, hedges.
- `hills_merge.gd`: `HillsMerge`: bakes static meshes into one mesh per material (draw calls).
- `gaps.json` (26 gaps), `goals.json` (11 goals).
- `bot_route.json`: generated by `make_route.gd` from the live level
  (`godot --headless --path . -s res://game/levels/hills/make_route.gd`). Rerun after moving anything.

## Notes

- Side streets hold their junction height for the through street's core width before they climb or drop
  (`_layout_roads`); without that the corridors fight and leave a kink that launches you at speed.
- People are all adults (docs/levels.md); the school zone is told by the crossing, beacons, signs, the guard, the
  school marquee, bikes on the lawns and a lemonade stand.
