# Beachfront (`beach`)

Golden-afternoon beachfront. The ocean is the star: the Ocean node rolls surf onto a wide sand beach, with
headlands, an island, sailboats and gulls out on the bay. A concrete skatepark, a boardwalk, a seawall ledge and a
108 m pier sit along the shore, with a palm-lined beach road and a town behind.

## Layout (world metres; +Z is out to sea, the waterline is z = 40)

| Area | Where | What |
|---|---|---|
| Beach road | z -60..-44 | 4 lanes of looping traffic (2 x 7 cars), parked surf vans and cars, palms on both sidewalks, a zebra crossing, the town and hills behind |
| Skatepark | x -110..-38, z -40..-8 | heightfield snake run (S-bend, 1.7 m deep, coping both rims), 12 x 9 m bowl, funbox, pyramid, park ledge and flat bars, west quarter pipe, two banks down to the sand |
| Boardwalk | x -38..110, z -40..-8 | surf shop + pizza shack (rooftop gap, roof-access ramp), arcade, rentals, ice-cream stand, food stands, benches, flat rails, manual pads, planters, the launch kicker |
| Seawall | z -9..-8 | 6 long concrete ledges (both edges grind), banks and lifeguard steps down to the sand |
| Beach | z -8..40 | 3 lifeguard towers, umbrellas, a volleyball net, driftwood log (grind), Rock Hop kicker, surfers and beachgoers |
| Pier | x 50..58, z -8..101 | plank deck on pilings, railings that grind end to end, a missing-planks gap, a fishing platform and a quarter pipe at the end |

Spawn: (0, 0.1, -27) on the boardwalk promenade, facing the sea.

## Files

- `beach_level.gd`: the level (`BeachLevel extends Level`), built in code in `_ready`.
- `beach_snake.gd`: `BeachSnake`, the snake run's centre line, heightfield mesh/collision, coping rails and lips.
- `beach_town.gd`: `BeachTown`, the town rows, hills, traffic loops, parked cars, surfboards (and `SurfCarry` for
  pedestrians), drifting sailboats.
- `beach_facade.gdshader`: one MultiMesh of procedural building facades for the town.
- `beach_merge.gd`: `BeachMerge`, bakes code-built props into one mesh per material (draw-call budget).
- `gaps.json` (17 gaps), `goals.json` (11 goals).
- `bot_route.json`: generated by `make_route.gd` from the live zones
  (`godot --headless --path . -s res://game/levels/beach/make_route.gd`). Rerun it after moving pieces.
- `beach_shots.gd`: render script for the review shots
  (`xvfb-run -a -s "-screen 0 1280x720x24" godot --rendering-driver opengl3 --path . -s res://game/levels/beach/beach_shots.gd -- --scripted <out dir> [view ...]`).
- Tests: `tests/unit/test_level_beach.gd`.
